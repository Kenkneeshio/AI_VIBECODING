HOW IT WORKS

A little room to play.

Design begins with the furnished image originally used for your Home Assistant Second Floor dashboard. Unchanged areas keep its source pixels; movable objects use cutouts from that artwork. Reference shows the starting image.

  • Objects: search the catalog or choose a category, then click the plan to place one. Drag an object to move it, drag a corner to resize it, and use the Inspector for position, rotation, color, and device links.
  • Artwork: choose Focus canvas to expand the plan; click the zoom percentage to inspect the art at actual size.
  • Lights: each room has a movable fixture. Enter its Home Assistant light or switch entity in the Inspector so the fixture itself becomes the dashboard control.
  • Camera: place a Camera thumbnail, assign camera and event-image entities, then drag and resize its frame. Latest event shows a still; Live uses a camera stream only when Home Assistant provides one.
  • Floors and rooms: choose a finish, then click a room; drag room corners in Room mode.
  • Walls: choose a finish and click any wall. Draw and move interior segments; exterior walls can be refinished while their illustrated outline stays fixed.
  • Layouts: browser edits save locally. When connected, the current Home Assistant layout loads on opening; a different browser draft remains recoverable. Export to dashboard to keep your design as the official plan, or use JSON to move it between browsers.
  • Home Assistant: Import from dashboard loads its saved editable layout. Export to dashboard sends this design, image, and controls to your Second Floor dashboard. Transfers happen when you press a button. PNG and YAML downloads remain available for manual setup.
